## Intent

A second DemoBench video for social media: more dynamic, Swiss-design-inspired motion graphics.
Get straight to the action — no title card. Same four points as the first video (unfamiliar
instruction set and architecture, tight limits, creativity, graphics and sound design) and the
same demos (Pelican, Carpet, LIMIT SET, Daybreak), plus the architecture diagram and the code/RAM
limits. Daybreak's own music.

- Daybreak soundtrack: from the kick (7.5 s) through the drop (to 45 s), then its outro (52.5–60 s).
